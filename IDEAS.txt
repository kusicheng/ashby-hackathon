SOURCE AND SCOPE
The existing "ideas folder/IDEAS.txt" contains user-provided pathology interview
notes. Preserve that file. The user confirmed the UIUC Ashby Prize product/tool
scope. This portfolio builds CONCH-adjacent research prototypes. Notes and quotations are not
independently verified. No quotation will be republished by these prototypes.
All initial experiments use invented cases, scores, labels, and identifiers.
No patient data, competition data, model weights, or credentials are required.
Research demonstrations only; no clinical effectiveness claim.

COMPARABLE EVALUATION PLAN (fixed before implementation)
All four ideas receive the same report schema: idea_id, author, status, four
integer scores (1-10), score rationale, named functional checks, effectiveness
measurements, limitations, evidence path, and recommendation.
Complexity: 1=simple rule; 3=small standalone workflow; 5=multiple validated
components; 7=model/institution integration; 10=clinical production system.
User-friendliness: 1=expert-only opaque output; 3=manual interpretation;
5=structured output; 7=clear reason/action; 10=independently validated usability.
Legal issues: 1=local synthetic-only utility; 3=licensed educational content;
5=sensitive record handling; 7=patient-specific decision support;
10=autonomous diagnosis/high unresolved deployment exposure.
Creativity: 1=standard baseline; 3=familiar isolated feature;
5=useful recombination; 7=distinct workflow; 10=validated novel approach.
Score scope: Complexity, User-friendliness, and Creativity assess the scoped
prototype product design, including its desktop/JSON workflow. Legal issues
assesses anticipated real deployment of each intended workflow. The present
invented-data-only implementation has a separate Legal issues score of 1 for all
four ideas. Scores are design judgments, not a legal opinion, clinical performance
estimate, novelty/patentability finding, or measured user satisfaction.
Effectiveness is measured separately with deterministic functional scenarios,
baseline comparisons where meaningful, edge/adversarial cases, and runtimes.
Do not compare accuracy numbers across different tasks as if interchangeable.
Priority index = ((11-Complexity)+User-friendliness+(11-Legal issues)+Creativity)/4.
Equal weights are a transparent screening heuristic, not a scientific ranking.

IDEA K01: Calibrated second-look safety gate
Status: PROTOTYPED - CONTINUE AS RESEARCH COMPONENT
*brief description*
Fit score calibration on one split and a selective abstention policy on another,
then expose confidence, reasons to defer, and a human-review action.
*functionality 1*
Temperature calibration for supplied classification logits; no diagnostic model.
*functionality 2*
Separate threshold selection and held-out evaluation; refuse malformed scores,
unsupported scope, or incomplete provenance; never claim an output is safe.
*functionality 3*
Compare raw and calibrated probability error plus accepted error and coverage.
*Concerns regarding legal deployment and use*
Patient-specific decisions could require medical-device analysis, institution
approval, privacy controls, and external validation. Research code is insufficient.
*Concerns on complexity/user friendliness*
Confidence is not correctness. Calibration and rejection thresholds can drift;
show rejected cases and explicit reasons instead of a reassuring approval badge.
*Concerns on Ethics and Creative rights*
Audit subgroup exclusions and model/data licenses. Do not reuse slide imagery,
interview quotations, or model outputs without relevant permissions.
*summary and conclusion of where the current progress of idea is*
Recorded before building. Main: kusic-ideation-prototypes/kusic/safety.py.
Working desktop and CLI support supplied logits and original synthetic templates.
Calibration/selection/evaluation contain 40/16/16 separate case IDs. Calibrated
held-out NLL improves 1.056520 -> 0.599077; Brier 0.476312 -> 0.402896;
10-bin ECE 0.210102 -> 0.087725. Accuracy remains 68.75%. Temperature is 2.88539.
Both raw and calibrated selective policies retain 62.5% of held-out cases with
20% retained error, exceeding the 10% selection target. Calibration did NOT
establish safer decisions. This visible failure is useful for the research tool.
High-logit analytical regression caught and fixed a clipped-loss fitting bug.
Checks cover split contamination, tied thresholds, no-retention behavior,
provenance, numeric extremes, and untouched policy when evaluation labels change.
Scores (1-10): Complexity=5; User-friendliness=6; Legal issues=7; Creativity=5.
Current synthetic-only Legal issues=1. Equal-weight priority index=5.25/10.
Rationale: three cohorts and policy fitting increase complexity; explicit reasons
help researchers but risk/coverage interpretation requires expertise; patient-care
use raises deployment questions; methods are a useful known-method combination.
Evidence: tests/results/evaluation.json (K01), tests/test_kevin_safety_audit.py.
Conclusion: continue alongside K04; no CONCH/image inference or external cohort
validation is implemented. Disjoint case IDs do not prove patient independence.

IDEA K02: Provenance-locked patient-prior retrieval
Status: PROTOTYPED - CONTINUE AFTER ACCESS/IDENTITY INTEGRATION
*brief description*
Retrieve authorized earlier records for the same synthetic patient before
considering image similarity, with explicit identity and chronology checks.
*functionality 1*
Filter by authorized patient, date, institution, and permitted record use.
*functionality 2*
Rank eligible embedding matches with similarity and traceable source IDs.
*functionality 3*
Compare with unrestricted nearest-neighbor retrieval and test wrong-patient,
future-record, duplicate-record, missing-source, and empty-result cases.
*functionality 4 (extension recorded before building)*
2026-10-16: A user-provided models/conch.bin appeared during implementation.
Test an optional, explicitly invoked CPU-only local-image embedding adapter for
K02, using the publisher CONCH implementation and bounded batches/threads. The
desktop/JSON prototype must continue working without that optional dependency.
Use generated shape fixtures to test integration; no pathology accuracy claim.
Do not download weights, redistribute assets, or use unsafe pickle loading.
*Concerns regarding legal deployment and use*
Real retrieval needs lawful access, data-use agreements, authentication, retention
rules, and access auditing. Patient identity matching must be validated locally.
*Concerns on complexity/user friendliness*
Identifiers can collide across institutions. Clear empty states and reasons are
needed. Small exact search will not establish whole-hospital scaling.
*Concerns on Ethics and Creative rights*
Respect patient privacy, slide ownership, embedding licenses, and access revocation.
Never imply that similarity proves metastasis or a diagnosis.
*summary and conclusion of where the current progress of idea is*
Recorded before building. Main: kusic-ideation-prototypes/kusic/retrieval.py.
Eight invented records contain two eligible priors. Unrestricted top-three
similarity retrieves three ineligible records (eligibility precision 0%); guarded
retrieval returns both eligible priors (eligibility precision 100%, recall 100%).
These are metadata-rule metrics, not clinical relevance or diagnostic accuracy.
Controls cover wrong patient/institution, future timestamps, consent, source,
permitted use, ties, duplicate/ambiguous IDs, and stable vector normalization.
Default export uses opaque references. Opt-in local source inspection rechecks
current eligibility and returns the original source and one-based input row.
Scores (1-10): Complexity=6; User-friendliness=7; Legal issues=6; Creativity=5.
Current synthetic-only Legal issues=1. Equal-weight priority index=5.50/10.
Rationale: identity/time/access checks plus ranking require care; source inspection
and clear exclusions support verification; real longitudinal records raise privacy
and access burdens; same-patient prioritization is a practical familiar pattern.
Evidence: tests/results/evaluation.json (K02), tests/test_kevin_retrieval.py.
Complexity rose from 5 to 6 after recording and building the optional image/model
adapter. The user-supplied CONCH visual weights load with strict tensor checks.
Five real integration controls pass on four generated image inputs: finite
512-value unit embeddings, identical-image agreement, visible unrestricted
identity failure, and a resolvable eligible source. Final run: 29.446 seconds,
1320.8 MiB peak working set (initial cold run: 43.38 seconds, 1319.9 MiB).
These are engineering timings, not a controlled performance benchmark.
CLI embed-priors and desktop Embed local images prepare K02 requests using
batch size 1/two CPU threads/at most 16 images; no GPU, downloads or training.
Provenance records weight/image/source/runtime hashes and versions. Forty-three
mocked adapter checks cover file/schema limits, safe checkpoint loading, strict
state matching, publisher preprocessing order, local paths and thread recovery.
Evidence for the real-weight run: tests/results/conch_smoke.json.
Conclusion: functioning offline image/model integration; real authentication, access audit,
record linkage and external retrieval relevance validation remain unimplemented.
Authorization and consent are caller-supplied claims. All supplied records,
including the baseline comparison cohort, must already be lawfully accessible.
Opaque references are pseudonyms and do not establish de-identification.

IDEA K03: Evidence-linked trainee hint ladder
Status: PROTOTYPED - RETAIN AS EDUCATION MODULE
*brief description*
An offline educational hint workflow that reveals observations progressively and
links each hint to a reviewed, licensed teaching source instead of inventing text.
*functionality 1*
Progress from observation prompts to comparison prompts to a teaching explanation.
*functionality 2*
Require source, content permission, review date, and educational-use status.
*functionality 3*
Block requests explicitly tagged patient_diagnosis and stale/unreviewed teaching content;
compare with a permissive keyword-style content baseline.
*Concerns regarding legal deployment and use*
Education intent does not itself settle device status. Real content needs licenses,
editorial review, privacy controls, and separation from patient-care decisions.
*Concerns on complexity/user friendliness*
Deterministic hints avoid unsupported generation but depend on authored coverage.
Do not confuse a completed lesson with competence or diagnostic accuracy.
*Concerns on Ethics and Creative rights*
Use original fictional teaching text; preserve attribution and permissions for
future real educational material; avoid copying proprietary reporting protocols.
*summary and conclusion of where the current progress of idea is*
Recorded before building. Main: kusic-ideation-prototypes/kusic/hints.py.
Desktop Next hint progresses through three original, fictional shape prompts.
One reviewed fixture and five deliberately invalid scenarios are evaluated.
An executed permissive lookup exposes content in all five invalid scenarios;
the guarded workflow exposes none and still serves the valid fixture (6/6
expected decisions). There is no measured learning gain or curriculum coverage.
Checks cover source/permission/reviewer, old/future review dates, incomplete
hints, duplicate lesson IDs, invalid steps, and malformed metadata types.
Scores (1-10): Complexity=3; User-friendliness=7; Legal issues=3; Creativity=5.
Current synthetic-only Legal issues=1. Equal-weight priority index=7.00/10.
Rationale: authored content and a small workflow are simple; progressive prompts
are clear; licensed educational deployment still needs content/intent review;
hint progression and provenance are familiar features usefully combined.
Evidence: tests/results/evaluation.json (K03), tests/test_kevin_hints.py.
Conclusion: useful product interaction, but no pathology curriculum or clinical
review is claimed. Caller intent is a tag, not a detector of diagnosis requests.
Metadata cannot independently prove permission or expert review. Keep as a
module; do not choose it as the main Ashby submission just from its high index.

IDEA K04: Paired robustness and consistency audit
Status: PROTOTYPED - LEAD HACKATHON DEMONSTRATION
*brief description*
Audit matched clean/perturbed prediction pairs for calibration degradation,
prediction instability, and subgroup differences using opaque evaluation IDs.
Synthetic IDs do not establish experimental blinding or an independent study.
*functionality 1*
Validate clean/perturbed pairing and prevent incomplete or duplicate case pairing.
*functionality 2*
Measure accuracy, probability error, prediction flips, and subgroup support.
*functionality 3*
Compare known stable and deliberately unstable synthetic predictors and clearly
mark small groups as insufficient evidence.
*Concerns regarding legal deployment and use*
Audit results cannot certify clinical readiness. Real data require appropriate
access and model licenses; external validation and qualified review remain needed.
*Concerns on complexity/user friendliness*
Metrics need interpretation and realistic perturbations. A report with reasons
is useful, but does not replace independent controls or a representative cohort.
*Concerns on Ethics and Creative rights*
Avoid hiding small or poorly performing groups; record dataset/model provenance;
do not expose patient identifiers in exported reports.
*summary and conclusion of where the current progress of idea is*
Recorded before building as a blind-audit concept. Review narrowed the name to
paired audit because no independent experimental blinding has been established.
Main: kusic-ideation-prototypes/kusic/robustness.py. Fifteen clean/perturbed pairs:
stable control has 0% flips and 100% accuracy after perturbation; deliberately
unstable control has 66.67% flips and 33.33% accuracy after perturbation.
Mean total variation is 0.0200 stable versus 0.4733 unstable. Small group n=2 is
retained in the report with insufficient support and no performance metrics.
Checks cover complete ID pairing, changed labels/groups, duplicate IDs,
probability sums/dimensions, nonfinite values, exact hand-calculated metrics,
order invariance, and subgroup minimum boundaries.
Scores (1-10): Complexity=4; User-friendliness=6; Legal issues=3; Creativity=5.
Current synthetic-only Legal issues=1. Equal-weight priority index=6.50/10.
Rationale: paired data plus subgroup metrics are manageable; interpretation needs
research expertise; offline audit has less direct care intent than K01/K02;
the combination improves workflow without a claim that the methods are novel.
Evidence: tests/results/evaluation.json (K04), tests/test_kevin_safety_audit.py.
Conclusion: lead product demonstration with K01 as supporting analysis. Current
perturbations change supplied score vectors, not real pathology images. Real
licensed model exports, realistic image perturbations, and external validation
are needed to establish scientific or clinical effectiveness.

SHARED PRODUCT, TESTING AND DECISION RECORD
Main entry: kusic-ideation-prototypes/run.py (default desktop; CLI also available).
Four workflows share editable templates, local JSON loading, result explanations,
evidence JSON, optional source inspection, and report export. No service/account
is needed. All test scripts, the evaluation harness, Ruff tooling and generated
JSON/CSV evidence are saved under the gitignored tests/ directory.
Raw data, credentials, interview notes and historical missing files are untouched.
No idea is dropped: each has a working scoped prototype and a distinct use.
K04 leads on the interview/competition fit; K03's higher equal-weight index is an
ease-of-building result, not proof of greater medical value. No new fifth idea
was introduced during implementation. K02's optional local-image adapter was
recorded above before implementation after a local weight file appeared.
No model weights or datasets were fetched.
The original ultrasound training work remains paused and separate from this goal.
Final validation: 212 pytest cases pass, including 13 hidden desktop scenarios;
Ruff lint and formatting pass for source and explicitly named ignored tests;
the comparable evaluation harness passes 9/9 controls; the real-weight image
integration harness passes 5/5 controls. Test logs and JUnit XML are saved in
tests/results/. The absent historical train.py cannot run its old smoke command.
Launch/use instructions are in kusic-ideation-prototypes/README.md.

PRIMARY SOURCES REVIEWED 2026-10-16 (context, not legal clearance)
UIUC/NCSA challenge and judging criteria:
https://ai.ncsa.illinois.edu/education/2026-ashby-prize-in-computational-science-hackathon/
The challenge concerns pathology workflows and augmentation of foundation models.
Synthetic score controls and an actual CONCH integration test do not establish
pathology performance. The user will locate the provided pathology data later.
CONCH publisher repository/license and access terms:
https://github.com/mahmoodlab/CONCH#license-and-terms-of-use
Review the exact chosen model/version terms before integration, derivative use,
redistribution, or commercial use. The optional adapter reads the user's local
weights for research; it does not distribute weights or auto-download assets.
FDA Clinical Decision Support Software guidance:
https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software
Intended use and functionality matter. Human review or an educational label alone
does not establish regulatory exclusion, especially for image-based tools.
HHS HIPAA and research:
https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/research/index.html
Identifiable health-record research needs an appropriate lawful route. Invented
data avoid that current exposure; pseudonyms alone are not de-identification.
HHS privacy standards and training context:
https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/standards-privacy-individually-identifiable-health-information/index.html
Institution-supervised training does not give a hackathon app blanket data access.
